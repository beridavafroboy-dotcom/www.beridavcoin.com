2.1 Structural Virology & Entry Inhibition

The target pathogen is the single-stranded, negative-sense RNA genome of the Filoviridae family, focusing exclusively on the surface trimeric Glycoprotein (GP). GP is the sole engine mediating viral attachment and endosomal membrane fusion with host cell receptors.

  • Mechanism of Action (MOA): High-throughput docking engines are programmed to model a synthetic, non-toxic peptide derivative that binds tightly within the prefusion conformation pocket of the EBOV GP, preventing viral entry into the host cell.

2.2 Host Cellular Immunomodulation & Endothelial Stability

The profound bleeding and vascular leakage observed during severe clinical manifestations are caused by systemic inflammatory cascades. The virus primarily targets tissue-resident macrophages and dendritic cells, triggering a hyper-inflammatory "cytokine storm."

  • Mechanism of Action (MOA): Purified, isolated small molecules derived from targeted pharmacognosy are introduced to down-regulate the nuclear factor kappa B (NF-κB) pathway within infected cell lines, halting the overproduction of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-6, and IL-1β) to preserve vascular integrity.

In Silico Preclinical Discovery Framework
Diagram showing the in silico drug discovery pipeline, moving from target identification and structural biology to lead optimization and predictive toxicology.
  • In Silico Screening & Hit Identification
    Computational Modeling

    Processing genomic datasets using Pandas, NumPy, and R (Bioconductor). High-affinity compounds are structurally labeled, checked against predictive ADME/Tox matrices, and filtered to eliminate potential cardiotoxicity or hepatotoxicity before moving to chemical synthesis.

  • In Vitro Pseudovirus Assays
    BSL-2 Safety Protocols

    Evaluating lead synthetic peptides in cell culture against non-pathogenic, replication-deficient pseudotyped viruses (e.g., VSV or lentiviruses expressing EBOV GP) to verify real-world entry inhibition (IC50) under low-containment safety conditions.

  • Live Pathogen Neutralization & Efficacy
    BSL-4 Maximum Containment

    Testing finalized hits directly against wild-type Zaire ebolavirus strains inside an accredited Biosafety Level 4 (BSL-4) containment facility to measure cell survival, anti-viral latency, and real-world viral suppression profiles.
